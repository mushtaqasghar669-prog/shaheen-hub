About Shaheen Recall

Shaheen Recall is a lightweight productivity web application designed to help people capture useful information and retrieve it later. The first version focuses on speed, simplicity and local privacy rather than unnecessary complexity.

Privacy

In this version, saved Recall items are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. They are not sent to a Shaheen Recall database by this page. If cloud synchronization is introduced in a future version, this privacy information will be updated before that functionality is enabled.

Terms

Shaheen Recall is provided as a productivity utility. Users are responsible for the information they save and for ensuring that saved links and material are used lawfully and appropriately.

Contact

For questions, feedback or reports about Shaheen Recall, use the official contact address published with the deployed website.
